Daybook privacy

Daybook is a personal planner used by its owner.

  • Google sign-in is used only to identify you and keep your planner in sync across your devices.
  • Google Calendar access is read-only. Daybook shows your events next to your plans. It never creates, changes or deletes calendar events, and it does not store your events on a server.
  • Your tasks and notes are stored in a private Supabase database that only your account can read.
  • Ask Claude sends your question, your tasks and your upcoming events to Anthropic's Claude API to produce an answer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything else.

You can remove Daybook's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
